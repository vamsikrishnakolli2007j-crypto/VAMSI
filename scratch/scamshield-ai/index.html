Common Scam Types Directory

Learn the anatomy of prevalent scams affecting people everyday in India & worldwide

⚡

Electricity Power Cut Fraud

Scammers send fake SMS claiming power will be cut tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bills. They give a personal phone number and ask you to install AnyDesk or pay on unverified UPI.

  • Personal 10-digit mobile number
  • Panic deadline: "tonight at 9:30 PM"
  • Requests screen sharing or APK install
🏦

Bank KYC & Account Block

Claims your SBI, HDFC, or ICICI account will be suspended today because your PAN/KYC is outdated. Directs you to cloned phishing websites ending in .xyz or .top.

  • Unofficial domains (sbi-kyc-update.xyz)
  • Threat of immediate 24-hour account freeze
  • Asks for Internet Banking passwords or OTP
💼

YouTube / Task Prepaid Fraud

Unsolicited WhatsApp offers of ₹3,000–₹8,000/day for liking videos or rating Google maps. Pays ₹150 first as bait, then demands large prepaid deposits on Telegram.

  • Unrealistically high pay for trivial tasks
  • Channels users to Telegram secret groups
  • Demands "VIP task deposit" to release money
📦

Courier / India Post Smishing

Claims a parcel could not be delivered due to an incomplete street address. Prompts you to click a link and pay a tiny ₹10 fee, harvesting your full card credentials.

  • Fake tracking link (indiapost-track.top)
  • Urgent 12-hour deadline before return
  • Requests CVV and OTP for small token fees
👮

Digital Arrest & Legal Threat

Impersonates police, CBI, or customs officials claiming an illegal parcel or money laundering case in your name. Coerces victims over Skype or video calls to transfer savings.

  • Threat of "immediate arrest" or raid
  • No police force conducts arrests via video call
  • Demands "security deposit" to clear name
🎁

KBC / Lottery Lucky Draw

Claims your mobile number won ₹25,00,000 in a television lottery. Asks for bank details and GST processing fees to 'release' a prize that doesn't exist.

  • Prizes for contests you never entered
  • Advance tax or processing fee demands
  • Unofficial WhatsApp contact numbers
